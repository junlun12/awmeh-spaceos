## 1. System Integration: AWMEH Neuro-Affective & Physical Health Engine
Our software integrates official NASA open-science datasets into two complementary monitoring layers:
- **Layer 1: Neuro-Affective & Behavioral Resilience (AWMEH Engine)**: Passive detection of sleep disruption, neuro-inflammation, and cognitive strain in long-duration isolation (180+ days).
- **Layer 2: Spaceflight Physiological & Hazard Baselines**: Environmental radiation tracking and musculoskeletal degradation countermeasure calibration.

## 3. How NASA Data Directly Powers Our Software Features
1. **Sleep Architecture & Circadian Realignment**: Benchmarked against spaceflight and analog biological assays to detect REM latency anomalies and trigger adaptive cabin spectrum adjustments (480nm blue light).
2. **Neuro-Inflammation & Cognitive Strain Detection**: Utilizes cortical immunoassay datasets (e.g., OSD-751) to correlate physical biomarkers with psychological fatigue and emotional masking.
3. **Bone & Muscle Loss Countermeasures**: Employs spaceflight DXA microstructure data (e.g., OSD-661) to automatically calculate individual resistance exercise requirements.
4. **Radiation Risk Surveillance**: Telemetry thresholds calibrated against cosmic ray and solar particle dose-response records from NASA OSDR.
