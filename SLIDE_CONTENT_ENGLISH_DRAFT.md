## 📖 The Core Narrative
When humans travel to Mars, they will face 180 days of absolute isolation. Earth becomes an invisible pale blue dot. There are no resupply ships, no fresh air, and a 22-minute communication delay means **no real-time psychological hotline can ever save you**.

Astronauts are heroes with "The Right Stuff"—they train 15 years for this mission. When they feel exhaustion or creeping depression, **they hide it on self-surveys** because admitting distress means being grounded and disgraced. 

**awmeh SpaceOS** is not a punitive surveillance tool that reports them to Houston. It is a **silent, compassionate guardian** living locally on the ship's neural edge. It listens to the micro-tremor in their voice, notices when they stop eating in the galley, and quietly heals their environment (circadian lighting, acoustic sleep entrainment, and Apollo 13 historic anchors)—protecting their minds without asking a single survey or casting a single shadow of stigma.
